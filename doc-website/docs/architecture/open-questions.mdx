## 🟡 How much cache memory the engine needs

ElastiCache Serverless for Valkey was chosen ([ADR-03](./decisions.mdx#adr-03)), but its memory is the estimate we trust the least:

| What | Estimate | Depends on |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Idempotency | 18 MB (1 h TTL) to 430 MB (24 h TTL) | The TTL |
| 10–30 min windows | ~15 MB | Accounts active in the window |
| Account history (`out:` 24 h, `in:` 60 min) | ~260 MB of entries plus ~100 MB of keys | Measured at ~70 bytes per entry in Valkey: ~3.7 million live outgoing entries at 50 tx/s. Replaces the earlier 100–200 MB of 24-hour sums and ~100 MB of velocity history |
| Accounts and merchants (`acct:`, `merch:`, HU-67) | **~605 MB, measured** | ~261 bytes per account hash (currency, owner id, document, status, with its TTL) × 2.3 million accounts, plus ~1 MB for 5,000 merchants. Measured on 2026-11-02 by loading 200 thousand synthetic accounts with the cache loader into a local Valkey 7 and extrapolating to the full-mode seed |
| Restricted list cache (`{restricted}:docs`) | **~3.3 MB, measured** | 50 thousand documents in one set |
| Active rules | A few KB | — |

With the account history and the reference data of HU-67 the cache would be **~1 GB**. At $0.084 per GB-hour that is about $61 a month of memory, some $40 more than the ~$23 of the cost table (which assumes 300 MB); the cost table does not change until the load test confirms the history part. Ways to bring it down if needed: keep only the accounts with recent activity, or drop `user_id` from `acct:` (the engine does not read it today). To be confirmed with the load test. Also pending: the real latency of the serverless layer and the operations it consumes per transaction.

## 🔵 Cases consumer: Lambda or Fargate

All three queues are consumed by Lambda. If case processing turns out heavier, that consumer can move to Fargate (~$30/month per task). Decided by measuring, not estimating.

## 🔵 Real duration of the five Spark jobs

Frequencies are defined; durations are not measured. That decides whether EMR Serverless stays at $7.50/month or drops to about $4.

## 🔵 EKS against Fargate for the decision engine

Measured in week 5 with a short EKS deployment (see [ADR-06](./decisions.mdx#adr-06)): p99 latency at 50, 150 and 300 tx/s, deployment and operation effort, and monthly cost at production size.

## 🟡 Where the Kaggle history lives in production

Today the PaySim and Sparkov sample linked to the seed users is in the PostgreSQL `transacciones` table (loaded once, never edited), and the full CSVs are in `raw/kaggle/` in S3. Still to decide whether in production the sample also moves to S3 (and DynamoDB), so that all the history is in one place. Jorge sees S3 as the more feasible option (2026-10-19); for now it stays as it is.
